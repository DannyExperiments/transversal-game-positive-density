# Provenance

The vanishing-threshold conjecture is Conjecture 7 of Ranđelović’s The transversal game. The [recovered proof](proof/recovered-construction.md) gives the block construction and sharp fixed-pair count. The [companion](proof/companion-q1154.md) uses the published block strategy to address Conjecture 5.
