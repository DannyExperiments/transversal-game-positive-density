# Claim scope

Both players aim for the same family of transversals in the strong game. The block pairing prevents the first player from forcing a win. This gives a lower bound for the universal winning-size threshold.

The density optimum established here concerns families certified by a fixed collection of pairwise disjoint pairs. The unrestricted optimal defensive density is outside the claim. The separate exponential-size winning family follows from the published Maker–Breaker strategy after adding a common opening cell.
